AI in Action

Discover how Artificial Intelligence can be applied in education, business and healthcare.

01

AI in Education

AI can support education by providing personalised learning experiences, assisting educators and helping students access information.

Examples

  • Personalised learning activities
  • AI-powered learning assistants
  • Automated feedback
  • Educational chatbots
Example:

An educational platform can use AI to analyse a student's learning activities and provide suitable practice materials.

02

AI in Business

Businesses can use AI to support customer service, analyse information, automate selected processes and assist employees.

Examples

  • Customer service chatbots
  • Business data analysis
  • Automated document processing
  • Marketing analysis
Example:

A company may use an AI chatbot to answer frequently asked customer questions and direct customers to appropriate information.

03

AI in Healthcare

AI can support selected healthcare activities, including analysis of medical information, images and administrative processes.

Examples

  • Medical image analysis
  • Healthcare information systems
  • Administrative support
  • Medical research
Example:

AI-based systems can assist healthcare professionals with analysing medical images as part of a broader clinical process.

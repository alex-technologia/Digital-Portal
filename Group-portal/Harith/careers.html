Cybersecurity Careers & Professional Certifications

Cybersecurity offers many career opportunities for people who are interested in computers, networks, problem-solving, and protecting digital information. The following are some common careers related to cybersecurity.

Career Opportunities

  1. Information Security Analyst: Monitors computer networks and systems for security threats. They investigate security incidents, identify vulnerabilities, and help protect an organisation's data and systems.
  2. Penetration Tester: Also known as an ethical hacker, a penetration tester performs authorised attacks on systems and networks to find security weaknesses. The weaknesses can then be fixed before real attackers exploit them.
  3. Cybersecurity Consultant: Helps organisations improve their cybersecurity. Consultants may assess security risks, recommend security solutions, and help organisations create better security policies and practices.

Professional Certifications and Learning Pathways

Certification Main Focus Who It Is Suitable For Career Benefit
CompTIA Security+ Network security, threats, risk management, cryptography, and security fundamentals. Beginners and students starting a career in cybersecurity. Provides a strong foundation and can help prepare learners for entry-level cybersecurity roles.
Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) Ethical hacking, penetration testing, vulnerabilities, and common attack techniques. Learners interested in ethical hacking and penetration testing. Helps develop knowledge of how attackers find and exploit security weaknesses.
CISSP Security management, risk management, network security, and security operations. Experienced cybersecurity professionals who want to move into senior roles. Provides a recognised professional pathway for experienced cybersecurity and security management professionals.

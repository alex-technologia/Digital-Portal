Cybersecurity in Technology and Daily Life

Cybersecurity is used in many areas to protect information, systems, and people from cyber threats. Here are three examples of how it is used in the real world.

1. Business

Businesses use cybersecurity to protect customer information, employee accounts, financial information, and company data. For example, companies use passwords, multi-factor authentication, encryption, and firewalls to prevent unauthorised access.

2. Healthcare

Hospitals store sensitive information such as patient records, medical history, and personal details. Cybersecurity measures such as encryption, access controls, backups, and security software help protect this information.

3. Government

Government departments use cybersecurity to protect public records, websites, communication systems, and citizen information. Firewalls, encryption, security monitoring, and access restrictions help protect government systems from cyber attacks.

Examples of Cybersecurity in Action

Area Example Purpose
Business Multi-factor authentication Prevents unauthorised account access.
Healthcare Encrypted patient records Protects private patient information.
Government Firewalls and monitoring Protects government systems and data.

Careers and Professional Certifications

Systems Administrator (SysAdmin)

Manages and maintains an organization's servers, operating systems, and core infrastructure.

Certification: Microsoft Certified: Azure Administrator Associate (AZ-104)

IT Support Specialist

Provides technical assistance for employee hardware and software issues.

Certification: CompTIA A+

Backup Specialist

Responsible for creating and maintaining backup plans to ensure data recovery in case of system failures or disasters.

Certification: CompTIA Storage+

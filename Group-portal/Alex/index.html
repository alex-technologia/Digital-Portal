Operating Systems

Overview of operating systems

An operating system (OS) manages computer hardware and software resources. Its kernel handles tasks such as memory, processes, files, devices, and security. Examples include Linux, Windows, macOS, Android, iOS, and real-time operating systems (RTOS).
